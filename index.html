Privacy Policy — Best Time Windows to Train

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Best Time Windows to Train (“the app”) is a Garmin Connect IQ widget that suggests the best time to train based on the weather and your body’s readiness. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses and sends.

What the app accesses

  • Weather (network request). To give you a better rain and wind forecast, the app requests your approximate location (latitude/longitude) from your watch and sends it to the public weather service open-meteo.com to retrieve a local hourly forecast. Only the coordinates and the forecast request are sent — no name, account, e‑mail, device identifier, or any other personal information. open-meteo.com’s own terms apply to that request: open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • Location. Used only to fetch that weather and to compute sunrise/sunset. The app uses your last known position and does not turn on GPS for this. Coordinates are sent only to open-meteo.com and are not stored.
  • Health metrics (on device only). Body Battery, stress, recovery time and your sleep/wake schedule are read from your watch to estimate readiness. These are used only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Calendar (optional companion). If you install the separate Android companion app, only busy time blocks (start/end times) are sent from your phone to your watch. Event titles and details never leave your phone, and nothing is sent to us or any third party.

What we collect and store

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user account, no analytics, and no server of its own.
  • The only outbound network request is to open-meteo.com for weather, as described above.
  • All scoring happens on your watch. App settings are stored locally on your device and in your Garmin Connect app settings, like any Connect IQ app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data.
